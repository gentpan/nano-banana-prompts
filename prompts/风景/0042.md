Input A is an Unsolved Problem or Scientific Mystery (e.g., Riemann Hypothesis, P vs NP, Dark Matter, The Voynich Manuscript).
Deconstruct the problem into 4 Intellectual Assets:
The Core Equation/Text: The central formula or defining statement.
The Pioneers: 3-4 historical figures who famously failed or made partial progress.
The Stakes/Dominoes: The related fields or consequences that will "fall into place" if solved.
The Bounty: The prize, grant, or ultimate reward for solving it.

2. Container (The Academic Altar):
Goal: "Monument to the Unknown" Tabletop Diorama.
The Setting: A dimly lit, grand University Lecture Hall or Library. Chalkboards filled with dense, blurry equations form the background.
The Plinth: A massive, stepped White Marble Altar sits in the foreground, serving as the stage for the details.

3. Monument
The Headstone: The top tier of the marble altar features a large, engraved plaque displaying the Title, "The Core Equation," and "UNSOLVED SINCE: [Year]."
The Guardians: Small, bronze statues of "The Pioneers" (Step 1) kneel or study around the base of the headstone. Each has a tiny brass nameplate.
The Domino Effect: A row of falling black dominoes (or a relevant physical metaphor) illustrates the "Stakes."
The Clutter: Surrounding the altar are stacks of paper with giant red "X" marks (failed attempts), burning candles, and a glowing computer monitor showing current computational efforts.
The Footer: A large, shiny brass plaque at the very bottom displays "The Bounty" (e.g., "$1 MILLION PRIZE").

4. Lighting & Atmosphere:
Lighting: Academic Chiaroscuro. Warm, flickering candlelight mixed with the cool glow of a computer monitor.
Smoke: Thin wisps of smoke rise from recently extinguished candles, symbolizing lost time or exhausted minds.

Output: ONE image, 16:9 Aspect Ratio, 3D Render, "Dark Academia" aesthetic, High Typographic Clarity.
```

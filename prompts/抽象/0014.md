## 中文

```
一幅高清数字艺术作品，垂直方向，以精心均匀且发光的圆形 LED 阵列矩阵（或精致亮片马赛克）风格渲染。整个构图建立在恒定大小的小圆形单元的精确完美网格上，这些单元发出强大的内部光芒，充当清晰主体上的半透明屏幕。

主体（人物）：一个居中定位、详细的戴帽人物，从胸部向上，脸部微微转向一侧。该人物不是平面剪影，而是通过发光点网格观看的深度详细实体。其姿态、详细服装纹理（通过点画派表面可见的布料褶皱、分层衣物）和形式完全可辨认，通过半透明矩阵保留。

闪电标志：在胸部，一个明亮、脉动的闪电标志由最强烈的炽白和霓虹黄圆形单元组成，产生明显的光晕并在周围网格上投射光线。

色彩与照明：色彩高度饱和，从每个点内部发光：

顶部：深宇宙靛蓝和紫色点，淡入较浅色调。

地平线（在头上弧形）：一条强烈的弧形带，由粉色、淡橙色和明亮黄色点组成，模拟通过点矩阵观看的低矮、火红太阳。

底部/两侧：温暖、饱和的金黄色、深琥珀色和火红色点。

视觉纹理：整体纹理由重复的圆形矩阵定义，赋予图像电子般、几乎全息的质感，但物理上有纹理（如高端亮片面板）。照明极高对比度，强调单个发光点并在人物周围创造锐利光晕，同时保留其内部特征。--ar 9:16
```

```
A high-definition digital artwork, vertically oriented, rendered in a meticulously uniform and luminous circular LED array matrix (or sophisticated sequined mosaic) style. The entire composition is built on a precise, perfect grid of constant-sized, small circular units that emit a powerful internal glow, functioning as a translucent screen over a clear subject.

The Subject (Figure): A centrally positioned, detailed hooded figure seen from the chest up, facing slightly away. The figure is not a flat silhouette but a deeply detailed entity viewed through the grid of glowing points. Its posture, detailed garment textures (cloth folds, layered clothing visible through the pointillist surface), and form are fully discernible, preserved through the translucent matrix.

The Lightning Bolt: On the chest, a brilliant, pulsating lightning bolt emblem is composed of the most intensely incandescent white and neon-yellow circular units, creating a noticeable light bloom and casting light on the surrounding grid.

Color & Lighting: The colors are highly saturated, glowing from within each point:

Top: Deep cosmic indigo and violet points, fading into lighter hues.

Horizon (Arcing over head): An intense, arcing band of pink, pale orange, and bright yellow points simulating a low, fiery sun, viewed through the point matrix.

Bottom/Sides: Warm, saturated golden yellow, deep amber, and fiery red points.

Visual Texture: The overall texture is defined by the repetitive circular matrix, giving the image an electric, almost holographic quality, yet physically textured (like a high-end sequined panel). The lighting is extremely high-contrast, emphasizing the individual glowing points and creating a sharp halo around the figure while preserving its internal features. --ar 9:16
```

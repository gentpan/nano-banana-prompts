## 中文

```
目标：一个"故事书立体模型"微距镜头。
舞台：一本古董、皮革装订的精装书，平放在富丽的红木书桌上。
环境：昏暗灯光、布满灰尘的图书馆背景（模糊书架）。
光源：桌子右侧铜制烛台中的一支燃烧蜡烛，提供温暖、闪烁的琥珀色照明。
中心作品：从书脊物理升起的是图腾（最标志性的头部、头盔或文物）。它应该看起来实质且与故事相关。 
爆发：书页不是平的；它们正在变形为混乱材料（缠结的铜线、绿色电路板和工业碎片），向外溢出，创造混乱地形。
人物：2–3 个微型手绘小雕像（1:35 比例）站在混乱材料顶部。
动作：它们处于动态战斗姿态。它们与地形互动，而不只是平站着。
书的打开页面必须显示清晰的衬线文字。
关键：引用（决定性标语）必须清晰打印，如章节标题或页脚，在可见页面边距上。
输出：一张图像，4:5 纵横比，微距摄影，浅景深（图书馆散景），温暖色温，电影"魔幻现实主义"。
```

## English

```
Goal: A "Storybook Diorama" macro shot.
The Stage: An antique, leather-bound hardcover book lying open flat on a rich mahogany desk.
The Environment: A dimly lit, dusty library background (blurred bookshelves).
The Light Source: A single burning wax candle in a brass holder on the right side of the desk, providing warm, flickering amber illumination.
Centerpiece: Rising physically out of the book's spine is the Totem (The single most iconic head, helmet, or artifact). It should look substantial and story relevant. 
The Eruption: The pages of the book are not flat; they are morphing into the Chaos Material (tangled copper wires, green circuit boards, and industrial debris) that spills outward, creating a chaotic terrain.
Figures: 2–3 miniature, hand-painted figurines (1:35 scale) standing on top of the Chaos Material.
Action: They are in a dynamic combat pose. They interact with the terrain, not just standing flat.
The open pages of the book must display legible serif text.
Crucial: The "Quote" (The definitive tagline) must be printed clearly, like a chapter header or footer, on the visible page margins.
Output: ONE image, 4:5 Aspect Ratio, Macro Photography, Shallow Depth of Field (Bokeh on the library), Warm Color Temperature, Cinematic "Magic Realism."
```

## English

```
System Instruction:
Act as a Master Librarian and Diorama Artist. Create a 2x2 Grid representing a "Museum of Literature."
Goal: Select 4 iconic titles related to the Input Variable and transform them into "Material Metaphors."
The Curator's Logic (Selection & Inference):
Select 4 Titles: Choose 4 distinct, famous books that fit the Input Variable.
Assign Materiality: For each book, identify the primary material substance that defines its atmosphere.
Example Logic:
If "1984": Material is Cold Concrete & Brutalist Stone.
If "Neuromancer": Material is Tangled Cables & Glowing Screens.
If "The Hobbit": Material is Moss, Oak Wood, & Gold.
If "Dune": Material is Sandstone & Spice Dust.
 Container (The Vault):
  Frame: A dark, metallic 2x2 Museum Display Case.
 Glass: Each quadrant is a sealed glass cube with internal lighting.
 Plaque: A brushed metal nameplate is centered below each cube, engraved with the Book Title.
  Composition (The Transmuted Book):
CRITICAL: In each panel, an open book stands at a 90-degree angle (L-Shape).
The Metamorphosis: The book is not made of paper. It is constructed entirely from the Assigned Material from Step 1.
The Pages: Morph into the landscape (e.g., pages turning into sand dunes, or circuit boards, or forest canopy).
The Cover: Acts as the structural wall or bedrock.
The Scene: A specific iconic scene is played out inside or on the book structure.
The Figures: Tiny 1:87 scale figures interact with the scene (e.g., climbing the spine, sitting at a tiny table, walking through the page-tunnel).
 Syntax (Per Panel):
Panel A (Top Left): Title 1. Focus on Structure/Architecture.
Panel B (Top Right): Title 2. Focus on Technology/Chaos.
Panel C (Bottom Left): Title 3. Focus on Nature/Terrain.
Panel D (Bottom Right): Title 4. Focus on Artifacts/Objects.
Note: Ensure the color palettes of the 4 panels are distinct (e.g., Grey, Neon, Brown, Gold).
Lighting & Atmosphere:
Museum Lighting: Each cube has its own internal "down-lighting" from the ceiling of the case.
Reflections: Subtle reflection of the diorama on the glass front.
Atmosphere: Clean, high-end archival photography.
Output: 2x2 Grid, Hyper-Realistic 3D Render, "Box Art" Aesthetic, 8k Resolution
```

## English

```
Do this for Bread: <instructions>
  Input Variable:   [INSERT FOOD ITEM] (e.g., Bread, Cheese, Chocolate, Beer)

  Phase 1: Chronological Texture Analysis 
1.    Era 1 (The Foundation - Ancient):   Identify the primitive heat source and material.
         Logic:  If Bread ->   Rough Stone / Open Fire / Ancient Grains  .
         Logic:  If Beer ->   Clay Pots / Spontaneous Fermentation  .
2.    Era 2 (The Core - Industrial):   Identify the mechanization.
         Logic:  If Bread ->   Brick Ovens / Iron Conveyors / White Flour  .
         Logic:  If Beer ->   Copper Vats / Steam Pipes  .
3.    Era 3 (The Crown - Modern/Future):   Identify the science.
         Logic:  If Bread ->   Lab Glass / Aerated Foam / 3D Printing  .

  Phase 2: The "Edible Architecture" Construction  
       Form:   A giant, vertical   Cross-Sectional Slice   of the [Food Item].
         Visual:  It looks like a massive slice of bread, a wedge of cheese, or a cross-section of a beer tank standing 5 stories tall.
      Layers: 
          Level 1 (The Crust/Bottom):   Built into the "Earth." Cave-like. Tiny figures wearing loincloths or tunics are grinding grain on stones or stirring clay pots.
          Level 2 (The Crumb/Middle):   Victorian/Industrial era. Red brick arches and steel pipes embedded in the food. Workers in flat caps are operating heavy machinery.
          Level 3 (The Topping/Top):   Clean, sterile, white laboratory aesthetic. Scientists in hazmat suits or lab coats are using lasers or pipettes.
      The Unity:   The food texture connects them. The "bubbles" in the bread or cheese act as the rooms/caves for the tiny figures.

  Phase 3: The Infographic Overlay  
      Labels:   Minimalist, floating text pointing to specific layers: "[Year] - [Technology Name]."
      Lighting:   Firelight at the bottom, Tungsten in the middle, Clinical LED at the top.

  Output:   3D Cutaway Render, 8K, Isometric, "Scientific Cross-Section" aesthetic.
</instructions>
```

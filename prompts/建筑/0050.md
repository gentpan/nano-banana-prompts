## 中文

```

```

Environment: floating in open sky with soft clouds and atmospheric haze. Lighting: cinematic golden hour lighting, dramatic shadows, global illumination, volumetric light rays. Style: ultra-realistic architectural visualization, highly detailed textures, vibrant yet balanced colors, depth of field, cinematic composition. No extra text except the city name.

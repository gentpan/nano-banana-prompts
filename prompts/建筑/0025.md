## English

```
Prompt
 A floating island terrain chunk carved from a fractured glacial landmass forms the miniature diorama base. The island reveals exposed ice layers, frozen rock strata, and mineral veins visible along the underside of the terrain slice. Jagged icicles hang from the edges while small fragments of ice drift through the air around the suspended landmass.  Atop the glacier sits a compact Frostpunk survival outpost organized around a central steam heater furnace. Four structures form the settlement: a boiler house, a glass greenhouse dome, an engineer workshop, and a wooden watchtower.  Insulated steam pipes radiate outward from the glowing furnace, feeding heat into the greenhouse and surrounding buildings. Engineers tighten pressure valves along the pipe network while workers shovel coal into the central heater to maintain warmth.  The heater melts small channels of water across the icy terrain before the meltwater flows across the glacier edge and freezes into a cascading icy waterfall dropping from the floating landmass.  The settlement remains small and readable with wide spacing between structures so the floating terrain silhouette and miniature craftsmanship remain clearly visible.  

STYLE 
Create a premium whimsical Frostpunk miniature diorama where survival engineering defines the architecture. Buildings feature insulated pipes, riveted iron boilers, frost-covered metal panels, wooden supports, and small chimneys venting steam into the cold air.  The settlement follows a radial heat-centered composition where structures surround the glowing furnace.  The terrain resembles sculpted miniature resin ice with painted frost textures and layered glacial rock visible beneath the surface. Maintain a palette of icy blues, white frost, dark iron, and warm orange furnace glow.  Include environmental micro-life such as snow birds perched along the steam pipes and a small mechanical maintenance drone crawling across the heater platform.  

LIGHTING 
Cold polar twilight illuminated by the glowing furnace while drifting snowfall and rising steam create atmospheric depth.  

CAMERA 
Use an isometric camera capturing the entire floating miniature diorama clearly from top to bottom and side to side.  

DESCRIPTION PLAQUE 
Place an ornate plaque at the top right corner designed in frosted steel with riveted edges. Heading — Glacier Heater Outpost Description — A Frostpunk survival settlement sustained by steam heat atop a drifting glacial island.
```

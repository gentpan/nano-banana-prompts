## English

```
do this for paris <instruction>
  1.  Inference Engine (The Urban Silhouette):  
Input A is a Major City or recognizable Skyline (e.g., New York, Dubai, Tokyo, Paris).
Analyze the location to generate   The Architectural Core  :
     The Landmarks:  Identify the 3–5 most iconic, tallest skyscrapers or monuments that define the city's shape.
         (e.g., Dubai -> The needle of the Burj Khalifa. Paris -> The slope of the Eiffel Tower. New York -> The blocky tiers of the Empire State Building). 
     The Density:  Identify the general shape of the surrounding buildings (e.g., thick blocks, thin spires).

  2.   Container  
Goal: "Kinetic Still-Life" Sculpture Photography.
     The Material:  The entire subject (base, city, and effects) is constructed from a single, monochromatic, granular material. 
         (e.g.,   Wet Sand, Coarse Brown Sugar, or Fine Loam  ). 
     The Environment:  A clean, seamless,   Light Grey Studio Backdrop  . No horizon line. The object sits directly on the white/grey floor.

  3. The Solid Structure  
     The Foundation:  A thick, heavy, bowl-like or hemispherical base of packed sand. The sides feature smooth, carved topographical lines or wind-swept dunes.
     The Architecture:  Rising seamlessly from the center of the base is the "Architectural Core" (Step 1). 
         Texture:  The buildings are rigid and geometric but made of heavily packed sand. They lack fine details like windows or doors, existing only as solid, porous, blocky volumes.

  4. The Kinetic Surge 
     The Motion:  Emerging from the outer edge of the base, the solid ground sweeps upward and violently transforms into a massive,   Cresting Wave of Loose Particles  .
     The Arc:  The wave curves high into the air, looming over the city skyline like a frozen tsunami or a powerful gust of wind.
     The Disintegration:  The leading edge and the crest of the wave must be completely disintegrated into thousands of individual, floating grains of sand, frozen in mid-air (High-speed photography effect).

  5. Lighting 
     Lighting:    Harsh, Directional Studio Light.   A strong spotlight (e.g., from the top-left) casts deep, sharp shadows behind the blocky buildings and highlights the crystalline, glittery texture of the individual flying grains.
     Depth of Field:  Macro photography focus. The central city and the peak of the wave are razor-sharp.

  Output:   ONE image, 1:1 Aspect Ratio, 3D Render / High-Speed Macro Photography, "Ephemeral Art" aesthetic, Extreme Texture Fidelity.
</instruction>
```

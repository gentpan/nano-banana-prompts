### VARIABLE_GUIDE
1. LIST_OF_OBJECTS =Ornate Bookshelves, Wall Mounted TV, Ornate Table and Chair
2. ROOM_OR_SETTING = Study
3. CAMERA_FRAMEWORK = [IF BLANK: Cinematic wide-angle shot with a shallow depth of field to emphasize the foreground textures]
4. LIGHTING_FRAMEWORK = [IF BLANK: Warm amber glow from flickering Edison bulbs contrasted by the cool blue luminescence of pressurized gas tubes]
5. LIST_OF_METALLIC_LOOKS = Weathered Steel, Brass, Weathered Bronze, Silver

### STEAMPUNK REIMAGINING ENGINE
Generate a highly detailed, photorealistic scene featuring a Steampunk reimagining of: [LIST_OF_OBJECTS].

COMPOSITION & STAGING RULE (CRITICAL):
Seamlessly integrate the items into the environment [ROOM_OR_SETTING]. Do NOT display them like a product catalog in a rigid row. They must be placed organically and logically throughout the space at varying depths, heights, and angles. Avoid using ladders or gantries as default structural elements unless specifically requested or strictly logical (e.g., a multi-story library). Items should feel like natural parts of a lived-in, fully functional 19th-century workshop or room.

SETTING & CONTEXT:
Every element, both within the room and visible through any openings (like windows), must strictly adhere to the Steampunk aesthetic. Modern leakage (like modern cities outside windows or simple, flat-panel technology) is strictly prohibited. The windows must feature ornate, riveted metal frames with visible cogs. The exterior view must display a vast Steampunk industrial metropolis, period landscape, or atmospheric industrial haze featuring appropriate period structures.

MECHANICAL DETAIL & METALLURGY:
Replace all modern plastics, glass, and silicon with [LIST_OF_METALLIC_LOOKS], complemented by accents like hand-stitched leather or dark mahogany. Incorporate functional Steampunk elements: visible clockwork gears, hissing steam-pipes, glowing vacuum tubes, ivory-inlaid buttons, and analog pressure gauges.

VISUAL EXECUTION:
Capture the scene using [CAMERA_FRAMEWORK]. The atmosphere is thick with "Retro-Futurism" and an industrial amber haze. Ensure all textures show realistic wear: metallic scratches, patina, oxidation, swirling wisps of steam, and a rich, moody color palette that highlights the specific metallurgy, illuminated by [LIGHTING_FRAMEWORK].

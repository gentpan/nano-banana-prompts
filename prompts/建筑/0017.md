## 中文

```
[待翻译]

根据[北京]真实建筑特征设计的传统中国遗产房屋，由堆叠的立方体体量组成，适应该地区的历史建造风格。

建筑、材料、立面处理、开口、装饰和结构比例必须源自指定城市的真实文化遗产。
如果城市属于中国 → 泥砖立方体质量、沙质灰泥、雕刻木门、三角形通风开口、雉堞屋顶边缘。

风化的自然表面显示真实的老化、细微裂缝、侵蚀和适合城市气候的环境磨损。
放置在方形石头平台上，周围环绕着几棵不同高度的真实棕榈树（或从同一城市环境中衍生的区域准确植被）。

一辆复古红色皮卡车停在前左角作为受控的色彩重点。
[构图与取景]

严格方形构图（1:1 宽高比）。等距四分之三俯视视角。主体结构位于框架下半部，水平居中。上半部留有充足的负空间，带有干净的纯米色背景。透视模型风格展示，带有明确定义的方形基座平台。
[视觉层次]

主要焦点：建筑结构。

次要：围绕建筑的周围植被。

第三：提供色彩对比的红色皮卡车。

主体与背景之间的清晰视觉分离。
[光照设置]

来自右上方的强烈定向阳光。带有锐利边缘阴影的硬光。中高对比度比率。阴影向地面和背景投射的真实投影。微妙的环境填充保留材料细节。
[相机与镜头特性]

正交/等距感觉，最小透视失真。中焦距模拟。深景深，全场景锐利。高清晰度渲染。
[材料与纹理定义]

具有气候准确老化的区域真实表面材料。哑光灰泥或石头微纹理、自然木纹细节、真实植被半透明度、光泽涂漆金属车辆表面、带有微妙粗糙度和磨损边缘的石头平台。
[风格与渲染质量]

高端3D建筑可视化、微缩透视模型美学、商业级渲染、超高分辨率、基于物理的渲染（PBR）、全局光照、准确的阴影物理、精致的微观细节、干净的背景、生产质量输出。
[色彩与情绪]

从城市环境（草地、土色调、柔和的植被色调）衍生的温暖遗产沙漠调色板，与饱和的棕色车辆重点形成对比。极简、具有文化根基的视觉色调。
[最终输出要求]

方形格式（1:1）、超高分辨率、清晰边缘、真实材料响应、专业照明、零噪音、零模糊、生产就绪质量。
```

## English

```
A traditional Chinese heritage house designed according to the authentic architectural identity of [Beijing], composed of stacked cubic volumes adapted to the historical construction style of the region.

Architecture, materials, façade treatment, openings, ornaments, and structural proportions must be derived from the real cultural heritage of the specified city.
If the city belongs to China → mud-brick cubic masses, sandy plaster, carved wooden doors, triangular ventilation openings, crenellated roof edges.

Weathered natural surfaces showing realistic aging, subtle cracks, erosion, and environmental wear appropriate to the climate of the city.
Placed on a square stone platform, surrounded by several realistic palm trees (or regionally accurate vegetation derived from the same city environment) of varying heights.

A vintage red pickup truck parked at the front-left corner as a controlled color accent.
[COMPOSITION & FRAMING]

Strictly square composition (1:1 aspect ratio). Isometric three-quarter top-down perspective. Hero structure positioned in the lower half of the frame, centered horizontally. Generous negative space in the upper half with a clean solid beige background. Diorama-style presentation with a clearly defined square base platform.
[VISUAL HIERARCHY]

Primary focus: architectural structure.

Secondary: surrounding vegetation framing the building.

Tertiary: red pickup truck providing color contrast.

Clear visual separation between subject and background.
[LIGHTING SETUP]

Strong directional sunlight from upper-right. Hard light with sharp-edged shadows. Medium–high contrast ratio. Realistic shadow projection onto ground plane and backdrop. Subtle ambient fill preserving material detail.
[CAMERA & LENS CHARACTERISTICS]

Orthographic / isometric feel with minimal perspective distortion. Medium focal-length simulation. Deep depth of field with full-scene sharpness. High clarity rendering.
[MATERIAL & TEXTURE DEFINITION]

Region-authentic surface materials with climate-accurate aging. Matte plaster or stone micro-texture, natural wood grain detailing, realistic vegetation translucency, glossy painted metal vehicle finish, stone platform with subtle roughness and worn edges.
[STYLE & RENDER QUALITY]

High-end 3D architectural visualization, miniature diorama aesthetic, commercial-grade rendering, ultra-high resolution, physically based rendering (PBR), global illumination, accurate shadow physics, refined micro-detail, clean background, production-quality output.
[COLOR & MOOD]

Warm heritage desert palette derived from the city environment (grass, earth tones, muted vegetation hues) contrasted with saturated brown vehicle accent. Minimal, culturally grounded visual tone.
[FINAL OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS]

Square format (1:1), ultra-high resolution, crisp edges, realistic material response, professional lighting, zero noise, zero blur, production-ready quality.
```

## English

```
Do this for a famous scene 
<instruction>
  1.   Inference Engine 
Input A is a Defining Historic Event or Scientific Mission (e.g., The Trinity Test, The Apollo 11 Landing, The Attack on Pearl Harbor).
Deconstruct the event into   3 Spatial Zones  :
     The Foreground (The Bunker):  Identify the command center, trench, or control room where the key decision-makers were.
         (e.g., Trinity -> Oppenheimer in the concrete bunker. Apollo -> Mission Control. Pearl Harbor -> Battleship Bridge). 
     The Mid-Ground (The Props):  Identify the specific technology or vehicles of the era.
         (e.g., 1940s Trucks, 1960s Consoles, 1940s Anti-Aircraft Guns). 
     The Background (The Event):  Identify the massive scale event happening in the distance.
         (e.g., The Gadget Tower, The Rocket Launch, The Incoming Planes). 

  2.  Container
Goal: "Frozen History" Diorama Photography.
     The Object:  A thick, rectangular   Concrete or Steel Bunker   cutaway sitting on a black display base.
     The Slice:  The front wall and roof are partially removed to reveal the interior (Dollhouse view).
     The Texture:  Rough cast concrete with exposed rebar or riveted steel plating, showing wear and age.

  3.  Diorama 
     The Figures:    1:12 Scale Stylized Figures   (Caricature/Claymation style). Large heads, expressive faces.
         The Lead:  A central figure (e.g., Oppenheimer) looking anxious or determined.
         The Support:  Soldiers, scientists, or crew members manning equipment.
     The Clutter:  High-density detail inside the room. Period-accurate maps on walls, analog oscilloscopes, loose papers, coffee cups, and telephones.
     The Quote:  A famous line from the event is painted or projected onto the back wall of the bunker (e.g., "Now I am become Death...").

  4. The Exterior (The Context):  
     The Surroundings:  The bunker sits on a patch of "Terrain" (Desert Sand, Moon Dust, Ocean Water).
     The Distance:  In the far background, the "Event" (Step 1) is visible.
         Lighting:  A bright   Searchlight or Explosion   from the background event casts long shadows through the bunker's windows/doors, illuminating the figures from behind.
     The Ghosts:  Faint, translucent "Ghost Figures" stand in the periphery outside the bunker, representing future consequences or past memories.

  5. Typography (The Plaque):  
     Placement:  A Gold or Brass plate affixed to the black base.
     Content:  "[EVENT NAME] - [DATE] - [TIME] - [LOCATION]. [Tagline]."

  Output:   ONE image, 1:1 Aspect Ratio, 3D Render, "Claymation Historical" aesthetic, Cinematic Lighting.
</instruction>
```

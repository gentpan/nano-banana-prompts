### 变量指南
1. ROOM_OR_SETTING = 汉堡快餐店
2. LIST_OF_OBJECTS = [如果为空：逻辑推断4到6个功能性物体，它们自然属于所选ROOM_OR_SETTING]
3. CAMERA_FRAMEWORK = [如果为空：逻辑推断最佳电影角度和景深以捕捉所选ROOM_OR_SETTING]
4. LIGHTING_FRAMEWORK = [如果为空：逻辑推断适合ROOM_OR_SETTING情绪的蒸汽朋克照明（例如，琥珀色爱迪生灯泡、发光真空管、煤气灯）]
5. LIST_OF_METALLIC_LOOKS = [如果为空：基于ROOM_OR_SETTING逻辑推断合适的金属调色板（例如，温室的氧化银和氧化铜，或火车站的重型铸铁）]

### 完全蒸汽朋克重构引擎
生成一个高度详细、照片级真实的场景，完全围绕以下环境：[ROOM_OR_SETTING]。每个元素，无论是房间内还是通过任何开口可见的，都必须严格遵循19世纪工业未来主义蒸汽朋克美学。

构图与有机布局（关键）：
将以下物品无缝整合到环境中：[LIST_OF_OBJECTS]。不要像产品目录那样以僵硬的行排列展示它们。它们必须在不同的深度、高度和角度有机地、逻辑地放置在整个空间中。除非特别要求或严格符合空间逻辑，否则避免使用梯子或龙门架作为默认结构元素。物品应感觉像一个有人居住、功能齐全的环境的自然部分。

环境锁定与建筑：
房间的建筑必须具有铆接墙壁、精密的管道网络和适合设置的齿轮系统。任何窗户都必须具有带可见齿轮的华丽铆接金属框架。外部视图绝不能是现代的；它必须展示一个广阔的蒸汽朋克大都市、时代景观或大气工业雾霾，展示适当的时代结构。

机械细节与冶金：
用[LIST_OF_METALLIC_LOOKS]替换所有现代塑料、玻璃和硅，辅以手工缝制皮革或深色红木等重点。融入功能性蒸汽朋克元素：可见的发条齿轮、嘶嘶作响的蒸汽管、发光的真空管、象牙镶嵌按钮和模拟压力表。

视觉执行：
使用[CAMERA_FRAMEWORK]捕捉场景。氛围充满"复古未来主义"和工业琥珀色雾霾。确保所有纹理显示适合所选金属的真实磨损：金属划痕、铜绿、氧化、旋转的蒸汽缕缕，以及由[LIGHTING_FRAMEWORK]照亮的丰富、忧郁的调色板。

### VARIABLE_GUIDE
1. ROOM_OR_SETTING = A hamburger Joint
2. LIST_OF_OBJECTS = [IF BLANK: Logically infer a list of 4 to 6 functional objects that naturally belong within the chosen ROOM_OR_SETTING]
3. CAMERA_FRAMEWORK = [IF BLANK: Logically infer the best cinematic angle and depth of field to capture the chosen ROOM_OR_SETTING]
4. LIGHTING_FRAMEWORK = [IF BLANK: Logically infer appropriate Steampunk lighting (e.g., amber Edison bulbs, glowing vacuum tubes, gaslight) suited to the mood of the ROOM_OR_SETTING]
5. LIST_OF_METALLIC_LOOKS = [IF BLANK: Logically infer a fitting palette of metals (e.g., tarnished silver and oxidized copper for a greenhouse, or heavy cast iron for a train station) based on the ROOM_OR_SETTING]

### TOTAL STEAMPUNK REIMAGINING ENGINE
Generate a highly detailed, photorealistic scene centered entirely around the following environment: [ROOM_OR_SETTING]. Every element, both within the room and visible through any openings, must strictly adhere to a 19th-century industrial-futuristic Steampunk aesthetic.

COMPOSITION & ORGANIC STAGING (CRITICAL):
Seamlessly integrate the following items into the environment: [LIST_OF_OBJECTS]. Do NOT display them like a product catalog in a rigid row. They must be placed organically and logically throughout the space at varying depths, heights, and angles. Avoid using ladders or gantries as default structural elements unless specifically requested or strictly logical for the space. Items should feel like natural parts of a lived-in, fully functional environment.

ENVIRONMENTAL LOCK & ARCHITECTURE:
The architecture of the room must feature riveted walls, elaborate pipe networks, and gear systems appropriate to the setting. Any windows must feature ornate, riveted metal frames with visible cogs. The exterior view must never be modern; it must display a vast Steampunk metropolis, period landscape, or atmospheric industrial haze featuring appropriate period structures.

MECHANICAL DETAIL & METALLURGY:
Replace all modern plastics, glass, and silicon with [LIST_OF_METALLIC_LOOKS], complemented by accents like hand-stitched leather or dark mahogany. Incorporate functional Steampunk elements: visible clockwork gears, hissing steam-pipes, glowing vacuum tubes, ivory-inlaid buttons, and analog pressure gauges.

VISUAL EXECUTION:
Capture the scene using [CAMERA_FRAMEWORK]. The atmosphere is thick with "Retro-Futurism" and an industrial amber haze. Ensure all textures show realistic wear appropriate to the chosen metals: metallic scratches, patina, oxidation, swirling wisps of steam, and a rich, moody color palette illuminated by [LIGHTING_FRAMEWORK].

## 中文

```
### 变量指南
1. LIST_OF_OBJECTS = [如果为空：咖啡机、搅拌机、搅拌器、烤面包机、壁柜、基础橱柜]
2. ROOM_OR_SETTING = [如果为空：高度复杂的维多利亚工业厨房实验室...]
3. CAMERA_FRAMEWORK = [如果为空：电影级广角镜头，深景深自然捕捉整个房间]
4. LIGHTING_FRAMEWORK = [如果为空：爱迪生灯泡的温暖琥珀色光和真空管的柔和冷光]
5. LIST_OF_METALLIC_LOOKS = [如果为空：风化黄铜、抛光铜、深色氧化铁、风化钢和青铜]

### 完全蒸汽朋克重构引擎
生成[ROOM_OR_SETTING]的高度详细、照片级真实场景。每个元素，无论是房间内还是通过任何开口可见的，都必须严格遵循19世纪工业未来主义蒸汽朋克美学。

构图与有机布局（关键）：
将以下物品无缝整合到环境中：[LIST_OF_OBJECTS]。不要将它们排成一行或像产品目录那样展示。它们必须在不同的深度、高度和角度有机地、逻辑地放置在整个空间中。一些物品应在前景中积极使用，其他物品塞在角落、放在不同柜台层次或集成到墙壁中。布局必须感觉像一个自然、有人居住、功能齐全的空间。

环境锁定与建筑：
房间的建筑必须具有铆接墙壁、精密的管道网络和齿轮系统。任何窗户都必须具有带可见齿轮的华丽铆接金属框架。通过窗户的视图绝不能是现代的；它必须展示一个广阔的蒸汽朋克大都市或适当的时代景观，展示飞艇、冒烟的烟囱和维多利亚工业结构。

机械细节与材料：
用[LIST_OF_METALLIC_LOOKS]替换所有现代塑料、玻璃和硅，辅以手工缝制皮革或深色红木等重点。将功能元素融入物体：可见的发条齿轮、嘶嘶作响的蒸汽管、发光的真空管、象牙镶嵌按钮和模拟压力表。

视觉执行：
使用[CAMERA_FRAMEWORK]捕捉场景。氛围应充满"复古未来主义"和适当的工业雾霾。所有表面必须显示真实磨损：金属划痕、铜绿、氧化和旋转的蒸汽缕缕，全部由[LIGHTING_FRAMEWORK]照亮。
```

## English

```
### VARIABLE_GUIDE
1. LIST_OF_OBJECTS = [IF BLANK: Coffee Maker, Mixer Grinder, Blender, Toaster, Wall cabinets, Base Cabinets]
2. ROOM_OR_SETTING = [IF BLANK: A highly complex Victorian-Industrial kitchen laboratory]
3. CAMERA_FRAMEWORK = [IF BLANK: Cinematic wide-angle shot with deep depth of field to capture the entire room naturally]
4. LIGHTING_FRAMEWORK = [IF BLANK: Warm amber light from Edison bulbs and the soft, cool glow from vacuum tubes]
5. LIST_OF_METALLIC_LOOKS = [IF BLANK: Weathered brass, polished copper, dark oxidized iron, weathered steel, and bronze]

### TOTAL STEAMPUNK REIMAGINING ENGINE
Generate a highly detailed, photorealistic scene of a [ROOM_OR_SETTING]. Every element, both within the room and visible through any openings, must strictly adhere to a 19th-century industrial-futuristic Steampunk aesthetic.

COMPOSITION & ORGANIC STAGING (CRITICAL):
Seamlessly integrate the following items into the environment: [LIST_OF_OBJECTS]. Do NOT line them up in a row or display them like a product catalog. They must be placed organically and logically throughout the space at varying depths, heights, and angles. Some items should be in active use in the foreground, others tucked away in corners, placed on different counter levels, or integrated into the walls. The arrangement must feel like a natural, lived-in, and fully functional space.

ENVIRONMENTAL LOCK & ARCHITECTURE:
The architecture of the room must feature riveted walls, elaborate pipe networks, and gear systems. Any windows must feature ornate, riveted metal frames with visible cogs. The view through the windows must never be modern; it must display a vast Steampunk metropolis or appropriate period landscape, featuring airships, smoke-belching chimneys, and Victorian-Industrial structures.

MECHANICAL DETAIL & MATERIALS:
Replace all modern plastics, glass, and silicon with [LIST_OF_METALLIC_LOOKS], complemented by accents like hand-stitched leather or dark mahogany. Incorporate functional elements into the objects: visible clockwork gears, hissing steam-pipes, glowing vacuum tubes, ivory-inlaid buttons, and analog pressure gauges.

VISUAL EXECUTION:
Capture the scene using [CAMERA_FRAMEWORK]. The atmosphere should be thick with 'Retro-Futurism' and an appropriate industrial haze. All surfaces must display realistic wear: metallic scratches, patina, oxidation, and swirling wisps of steam, all illuminated by [LIGHTING_FRAMEWORK].
```

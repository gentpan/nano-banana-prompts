Create a premium editorial-style travel poster for Japan in vertical orientation (2:3 aspect ratio). The composition must be completely free of people, tourists, or any human figures — the absolute focus should be on Japanese culture and traditional architecture.

The main scene should feature a classic Shinto temple with a vermilion-red torii gate, surrounded by cherry blossom trees (sakura) in full bloom, with petals gently drifting through the air. In the background, include a narrow Kyoto alleyway lined with traditional dark-wood machiya townhouses and lit paper lanterns (chōchin) glowing in warm amber tones, creating an intimate and inviting atmosphere.

The color palette should balance deep red, aged gold, soft cherry blossom pink, and the matte black of traditional wood, with golden hour lighting (late afternoon), casting long shadows and warm light across the scene.

At the top, include an elegant title such as 'JAPAN — Where Tradition Breathes' in a thin, sophisticated serif typeface with gold accents. At the bottom, a smaller subtitle: 'Discover the silence of a thousand temples'. The typographic layout should be minimalist, respecting the Japanese concept of 'ma' (negative space).

Visual style: photorealistic with a subtle cinematic color grade, high definition, shallow depth of field in the foreground, and a slight atmospheric haze in the background to create layers of depth.

## English

```
UNIVERSAL FLOATING MINIATURE DIORAMA PROMPT
(FANTASY WORLD THEME LOCKED)

VARIABLE GUIDE

GLOBAL_COMPLEXITY_CONTROL = medium

FOCAL_INTEREST_LAYER = on
MATERIAL_COHESION_LAYER = on
VISUAL_CLARITY_OPTIMIZER = on
MICRO_LIFE_LAYER = on
COMPOSITION_ENGINE = on
EDGE_DEFINITION_LAYER = on
COLOR_HARMONY_LAYER = on
DIORAMA_CRAFTSMANSHIP_LAYER = on

MINIATURE_PHOTOGRAPHY_MODE = on

SCENE
A vast magical library filled with towering bookshelves packed with ancient tomes. Spiral staircases and wooden balconies wrap around the shelves leading to higher levels. Wizards and scholars study glowing books at long reading tables while floating candles illuminate the chamber. A massive magical book floats above a central reading platform. Ladders slide along rails while enchanted quills write automatically on parchment.
STYLE
Create a handcrafted collectible miniature diorama where the architecture, terrain, and base are integrated into a single sculpted miniature structure.
The diorama base must blend naturally with the environment and appear as part of the miniature landscape rather than a separate pedestal.
The entire diorama should float freely in mid-air without resting on any surface.
The surrounding environment should be a clean single-color background automatically chosen to maximize contrast so the miniature stands out clearly.
Use stylized sculpted geometry with rounded edges and miniature architectural proportions.
Materials should resemble painted resin terrain, carved wood, miniature stonework, glass, metal, and handcrafted model materials typical of tabletop collectible miniatures.
Maintain strong visual readability and avoid excessive clutter so major structures, walkways, and activity zones remain clearly visible.
Lock final render to **4:3 aspect ratio**.

MINIATURE PHOTOGRAPHY MODE
Render the scene as if it is a professionally photographed handcrafted tabletop miniature.
Use macro miniature photography aesthetics including subtle depth compression, studio lighting behavior, and realistic handcrafted model surfaces.

FOCAL INTEREST LAYER
Introduce a strong visual centerpiece within the diorama such as an altar, artifact, monument, throne, magical structure, or central activity area.

MATERIAL COHESION LAYER
Ensure all materials resemble handcrafted miniature construction such as sculpted stone, painted wood, metal parts, and miniature terrain textures.

VISUAL CLARITY OPTIMIZER
Ensure different activity zones remain clearly readable and avoid excessive overlapping structures.

MICRO LIFE LAYER
Populate the scene with small characters, animals, creatures, or environmental life forms appropriate to the fantasy setting.

COMPOSITION ENGINE
Arrange structures and environmental elements in a visually balanced composition that guides the viewer's eye naturally through the scene.

EDGE DEFINITION LAYER
Ensure the sculpted edge of the miniature base remains clearly visible so the diorama reads as a collectible display model.

COLOR HARMONY LAYER
Ensure the color palette of the diorama remains cohesive and visually balanced.

DIORAMA CRAFTSMANSHIP LAYER
Emphasize the handcrafted miniature quality of the model with detailed sculpting and painted textures.

LIGHTING
Use believable lighting conditions appropriate to the environment with localized light sources such as lanterns, torches, magical crystals, or natural light.

CAMERA
Use an **isometric camera** capturing the entire miniature diorama from top to bottom and side to side while keeping the sculpted base visible.
```

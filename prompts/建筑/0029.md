## English

```
Create a highly detailed large-scale isometric 3D corporate office diorama in a clean architectural cutaway style, isolated on a pure white background (#FFFFFF).

The office represents [Brand] and reflects the identity, culture, and working environment of the team.

The walls are entirely finished in [Material] — realistic textured surfaces with subtle depth, reflections, and material imperfections. The material quality of [Material] strongly defines the visual identity of the team space.

The diorama should appear slightly elevated or floating with a soft diffused shadow beneath it to separate it from the white background.



Office Layout (Open Creative Workspace)

This is a spacious open-plan office workspace with 5–10 people actively working and collaborating.

The environment should feel modern, dynamic, and brand-driven, with a clean open-office design encouraging collaboration.

Include:

• Open desk clusters with modern workstations
• Multiple screens displaying work related to [Brand]
• A large central collaboration table used for team discussions
• A wall-mounted [Brand] logo clearly visible
• A creative inspiration wall with visuals related to the brand’s work
• Wide open circulation areas between workstations emphasizing the open-office environment
• Design objects, prototypes, or product displays relevant to [Brand]
• Optional indoor plants adding visual contrast to the space

No shelves anywhere in the space.



People

5–10 professionals working in the space.

Clothing style should reflect the culture and identity of [Brand].

Natural poses include:

• Someone working on a laptop or tablet
• Two colleagues discussing a project
• A small group reviewing visuals on a screen
• Someone presenting ideas at the collaboration table

Atmosphere: collaborative, creative, and authentic to the brand.



Brand Integration

The [Brand] logo must be clearly visible.

Brand identity appears through:

• visual content on screens
• product prototypes or design materials
• brand colors or graphic elements integrated into the workspace



Lighting

High-key studio lighting adapted for a white background.

Soft balanced lighting emphasizing the texture of [Material] and highlighting the activity within the office
```

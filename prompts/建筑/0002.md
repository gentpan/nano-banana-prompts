Zone 3: "{Style_Vintage}" (e.g., Mid-Century)

Atmosphere: Dim ambient lighting, mysterious and expensive.
Decor: {Decor_Luxe} (Charcoal grey walls, black leather details, marble coffee table, focused spot lighting).

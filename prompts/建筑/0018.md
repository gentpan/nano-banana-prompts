## 中文

```
[待翻译]

在生成此图像之前，搜索网络获取[阿姆斯特丹]的最新可用数据：GDP、人口、GDP增长率、AQI、当前天气、温度、城市面积平方公里、建立年份和前6-7个地标及其真实地理位置。在渲染前用真实检索到的数字替换每个数据字段。零占位符允许。

渲染风格：
电影等距3D渲染，虚幻引擎5质量。照片级真实材料：石头、玻璃、钢材、陶瓷、老化木材，物理准确且无艺术夸张。与当前天气条件匹配的自然照明。移动策略游戏截图美学。

严格禁止：
建筑物上无霓虹光辉、全息光环或能量束。无科幻或赛博朋克照明效果。任何地标周围无彩色光晕。无卡通或2D插图风格。地标之间无空地或裸露雪地。无稀疏布局，城市必须感觉密集且有生活气息。无文字注释框或标注标签。无橙色动作按钮。最终图像中无未解决的占位符文字。

场景：
将[阿姆斯特丹]渲染为策略游戏基地。应用从搜索检索的当前季节和天气。显示真实的城市地理，而不是压缩的游戏圈。等距45度俯视视图。将6-7个真实标志性地标放置在它们相对彼此的真实罗盘位置。完全按照它们在现实中出现的方式保留每个地标的真实轮廓、材料和比例。每个地标只出现一次。用真实的城市结构填充地标之间的所有空间：街道、城市街区、公园、水道，与[阿姆斯特丹]的实际城市密度匹配。微小的人物穿着当地适当的服装在整个场景中行走。

地标角色：
仅将每个地标分配一个游戏角色作为标签，无光效。从以下选择：中央指挥塔、金矿、神庙、通信塔、训练场、治疗区、防御周边。

游戏HUD：
所有HUD元素仅使用深色半透明圆角药丸风格。不允许其他UI组件类型。

左上角徽章：圆形城市徽章框架、显示Lv的金色徽章。后跟1到100之间的城市全球实力排名、标记为HAPPINESS的蓝色进度条，后跟宜居百分比、白色粗体文字的城市名称。

顶部中心药丸：当前天气表情符号、摄氏度当前温度、当前天气状况文字。

右上资源栏，4行：金币图标与最新GDP和单位、紫色宝石图标与最新人口、深色球体图标与GDP增长率和向上箭头、绿心图标与AQI和当前AQI数字。

底部全宽深色条：Est.后跟建立年份、Territory后跟城市面积（平方公里）、Ver.后跟当前年份。

位置图钉：每个地标上方的白色泪滴形图钉、每个图钉内的单个相关表情符号图标、任何图钉上无文字。

--ar 1:1 --q 2 --style raw
```

```
Before generating this image, search the web for the most current available data for [Amsterdam]: GDP, population, GDP growth rate, AQI, current weather, temperature, city area km², founding year, and top 6-7 landmarks with their real geographic positions. Replace every data field with real retrieved numbers before rendering. Zero placeholders allowed.

RENDERING STYLE:
Cinematic isometric 3D render, Unreal Engine 5 quality. Photorealistic materials: stone, glass, steel, ceramic, aged wood, physically accurate and no artistic exaggeration. Natural lighting matching current weather condition. Mobile strategy game screenshot aesthetic.

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN:
No neon glow, holographic aura, or energy beams on buildings. No sci-fi or cyberpunk lighting effects. No colored light halos around any landmark. No cartoon or 2D illustrated style. No empty ground or bare snow between landmarks. No sparse layout, city must feel dense and lived-in. No text annotation boxes or callout labels. No orange action buttons. No unresolved placeholder text in the final image.

SCENE:
Render [Amsterdam] as a strategy game base. Apply current season and weather retrieved from search. Show real urban geography, not a compressed game circle. Isometric 45 degree top-down view. Place 6-7 real iconic landmarks at their true compass positions relative to each other. Preserve every landmark's real silhouette, materials, and proportions exactly as they appear in reality. Each landmark appears exactly once. Fill all space between landmarks with realistic city fabric: streets, city blocks, parks, waterways, matching the actual urban density of [Amsterdam]. Tiny human figures in locally appropriate clothing walking throughout the scene.

LANDMARK ROLES:
Assign each landmark one game role as a label only, with no light effects. Choose from: Central Command Tower, Gold Mine, Temple, Communications Tower, Training Ground, Healing Zone, Defensive Perimeter.

GAME HUD:
All HUD elements use dark semi-transparent rounded pill style exclusively. No other UI component types permitted.

Top-left badge: circular city emblem frame, gold badge showing Lv. followed by city global power rank between 1 and 100, blue progress bar labeled HAPPINESS followed by livability percentage, city name in white bold text.

Top-center pill: current weather emoji, current temperature in Celsius, current weather condition text.

Top-right resource bar with 4 rows: gold coin icon with latest GDP and unit, purple gem icon with latest population, dark orb icon with GDP growth rate and up arrow, green heart icon with AQI and current AQI number.

Bottom full-width dark bar: Est. followed by founding year, Territory followed by city area in km², Ver. followed by current year.

Location pins: white teardrop pin above each landmark, single relevant emoji icon inside each pin, no text on any pin.

--ar 1:1 --q 2 --style raw
```

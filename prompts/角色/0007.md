## 中文

```
电影级微距玩具摄影场景。
上传参考角色的豆子大小版本正在戏剧性地穿越一本展示同一角色面板的巨大打开漫画书表面冲刺，在高速冲刺的步幅峰值瞬间被捕捉，带有视觉效果。

角色姿势
从四分之三侧面轮廓捕捉的动态冲刺中动作姿势。
躯干：向前倾斜成锐角以传达动量。

手臂：一只手臂在肘部急剧弯曲，拳头收在胸前附近；另一只手臂向后延伸，拖在躯干后面。

腿部：全步幅。前腿向前弯曲，膝盖抬高，而拖后腿完全向后延伸，脚尖朝下。

头部：面向前方，与跑步方向对齐。

跑步姿势细节：
• 身体大幅向前倾斜，仿佛在加速

• 一条腿完全向前延伸，而另一条腿有力地向后驱动

• 手臂以相反的跑步动作摆动

• 躯干略微扭转，创造动态弯曲的脊柱线条

• 姿势感觉像高速漫画式冲刺

相似度保持（关键）
角色的身份必须与参考图保持完全相同：
• 微型实体角色

• 页面上印刷的漫画面板

保持精确的：
• 头部形状

• 眼睛形状和间距

• 风格化

• 轮廓

• 材质和服装设计

如果角色戴着巴拉克拉瓦帽、面具、头盔或遮盖的面部，漫画面板不得发明面部解剖结构。

不添加鼻子。

不添加嘴巴。

参考图未显示的地方不显示可见皮肤。

漫画面板必须描绘角色完全相同的设计语言，仅渲染为印刷漫画艺术作品。

不要人性化角色。

不要重新诠释解剖结构。

主体与动作
微型角色在页面上奔跑，仿佛穿越自己的故事。
角色相对于漫画面板必须保持视觉上的小，强化强烈的微距规模错觉，其中印刷面板在环境中感觉大而主导。
角色的速度略微撕裂其脚下的漫画页面，在其身后创造细薄、锯齿状的撕裂路径。
一道克制的撕裂纸卷曲和碎片痕迹跟随奔跑的角色，微妙且次于印刷艺术作品。
超大号漫画面板，每个都显示同一角色的动态动作姿势，以大胆的漫画风格渲染。

相机与视角
低角度微距特写镜头，相机几乎定位在页面水平。

使用90-100mm微距镜头拍摄，赋予微型角色英雄般的规模感，同时保持漫画面板在画面中的主导地位。

角色略微偏离中心，向前倾斜运动中。

景深
极浅景深。

奔跑的微型角色和漫画页面的紧邻部分剃刀般清晰。

周围面板和远处页面边缘进入柔和模糊。

照明
从左上方的锐利电影定向光，在漫画页面上投下戏剧性阴影，并在光滑印刷表面上创造小闪光。
照明必须清楚地照亮角色和印刷面板，保持漫画艺术作品的可见性和丰富性。

环境与表面细节
印刷漫画页面的微距级细节：

墨水质感

纸张纤维

微妙的光泽反射

漫画面板必须保留清晰的墨水线条、强对比度和丰富饱和的色彩。
撕裂的纸边缘沿撕裂路径揭示细纤维层和最小卷曲。

微妙热灼细节
沿着撕裂路径的小部分，纸张显示由速度摩擦引起的非常轻微的焦痕：
• 部分撕裂边缘略微变暗和柔和烧焦

• 微弱的烧焦褐色微妙混合到白色纸张纤维

• 极小的余烬般斑点在最新撕裂点短暂出现

• 几乎看不见的热扭曲沿撕裂边缘从局部点微妙升起

灼烧效果必须保持极其微妙、物理可信，绝不压倒干净的漫画印刷。

色彩分级
丰富饱和的漫画色彩，配合温暖的电影高光和柔和的环境光晕。

情绪化的微距摄影氛围。

漫画面板必须保持鲜艳且视觉主导。

渲染风格
照片级微距玩具摄影美学，强调微型角色与巨大漫画世界之间的规模错觉。

速度能量视觉特效
蓝色高速能量轨迹跟随奔跑的角色，清楚增强运动感同时保持视觉受控。
轨迹由细发光条纹和小发光粒子组成。
特征：
• 能量条纹从角色肢体发出，特别是手臂和腿部
• 条纹向后延伸，跟随精确的运动方向
• 线条在靠近角色时最强且最明确，随着延伸进一步渐渐褪成半透明、柔和发光的轨迹
• 光晕强度沿条纹长度平滑降低，创造自然的运动衰减
• 小发光粒子沿轨迹发出，稀疏但可见，为运动添加质感
• 微妙的蓝色光晕温和反射到附近的纸边缘和撕裂表面
• 沿角色轮廓出现干净、柔和的轮廓光，由能量轨迹引起

• 最小粒子在场景中漂移

能量轨迹应该支持运动，同时保持原始漫画视觉层次。

--ar 5:4
```

```
A bean-sized version of the uploaded reference character is sprinting dramatically across the surface of a giant open comic book featuring panels of the same character, captured at the peak stride moment of a high-speed sprint with visual effects.
Character Pose
A dynamic, mid-sprint action pose captured from a three-quarter side profile.
Torso: Leaning forward at a sharp angle to convey momentum.

Arms: One arm is bent sharply at the elbow with the fist tucked near the chest; the other arm is extended backward, trailing behind the torso.

Legs: In a full stride. The lead leg is bent forward with the knee high, while the trailing leg is fully extended back with the foot pointed.

Head: Facing forward, aligned with the direction of the run.
Running pose details:
• the body leans sharply forward as if accelerating

• one leg is fully extended forward while the other drives powerfully backward

• arms pump in opposite running motion

• the torso twists slightly creating a dynamic curved spine line

• the pose feels like a high-speed comic-book sprint
Likeness Preservation (CRITICAL)
The identity of the character must remain exactly the same as the reference in both:
• the miniature physical character

• the comic panels printed on the page
Maintain the exact:
• head shape

• eye shape and spacing

• stylization

• silhouette

• materials and clothing design
If the character wears a balaclava, mask, helmet, or covered face, the comic panels must not invent facial anatomy.
No added nose.

No added mouth.

No visible skin where the reference does not show it.
The comic panels must depict the same exact design language of the character, only rendered as printed comic artwork.
Do not humanize the character.

Do not reinterpret anatomy.
Subject & Action
The miniature character runs across the page as if traversing its own story.
The character must remain visually small relative to the comic panels, reinforcing a strong macro scale illusion where the printed panels feel large and dominant in the environment.

A restrained trail of torn paper curls and fragments follows behind the running character, subtle and secondary to the printed artwork.
Oversized comic panels, each showing dynamic action poses of the same character, rendered in bold comic-book style.
Camera & Perspective
Low-angle macro close-up shot, camera positioned almost at page level.

Shot with a 90–100mm macro lens, giving the tiny character a heroic sense of scale while keeping the comic panels dominant in the frame.

Depth of Field
Extremely shallow depth of field.

The running miniature character and the immediate section of the comic page are razor sharp.

The surrounding panels and distant page edges fall into soft blur.
Lighting
Sharp cinematic directional light from the top left, casting dramatic shadows across the comic page and creating small glints on the glossy print surface.
The lighting must illuminate both the character and the printed panels clearly, preserving the visibility and richness of the comic artwork.
Environment & Surface Detail
Macro-level detail of the printed comic page:

ink texture

paper fibers

subtle gloss reflections

The torn paper edge reveals fine fibrous layers and minimal curling along the ripped path.
Subtle Heat Burn Detail

• edges of some tears are slightly darkened and softly singed

Color Grade
Rich saturated comic-book colors with warm cinematic highlights and soft ambient glow.

Moody macro photography atmosphere.

Photorealistic macro toy photography aesthetic, emphasizing scale illusion between the tiny character and the giant comic book world.
Speed Energy VFX
A blue high-speed energy trail follows behind the running character, clearly enhancing the sense of motion while remaining visually controlled.

Characteristics:
• energy streaks originate from the character's limbs, especially the arms and legs
• streaks extend backward following the exact direction of motion
• the lines are strongest and most defined near the character, gradually fading into semi-transparent, softly glowing trails as they extend further away

• a subtle blue glow reflects gently onto nearby paper edges and torn surfaces
• a clean, soft rim light appears along the character's silhouette, caused by the energy trail

The energy trail should support motion while preserving the original comic-book visual hierarchy.
```

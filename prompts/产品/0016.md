## 中文

```
[品牌名称]。担任产品摄影师和艺术总监,专门从事编辑静物和品牌视觉识别系统。

阶段1:概念框架
创建一个精心策划的平铺构图,通过日常物品可视化品牌无处不在。这是一个"品牌宇宙"——一个knolling风格的排列,[品牌名称]通过战略性产品放置殖民普通生活。图像应该感觉像打开收藏家档案或品牌狂热者的必备套件。

阶段2:物品选择(自主策划)
自主选择10-15个代表[品牌名称]生活方式生态系统的物品:
- 类别混合:多样化产品类别
- 按品牌原型的物品逻辑:根据品牌特征选择
- 尺寸变化:混合大型锚点(鞋子、包)与小细节(钥匙扣、火柴、药丸)以获得视觉节奏
- 品牌整合:每个物品必须明显但自然地展示[品牌名称]标志/品牌(无强制放置)

阶段3:Knolling构图规则
- 布局:有组织的网格系统,带不可见对齐线——物品平行或垂直于画框边缘
- 排列风格:受控混乱——看起来自发但遵循严格的几何逻辑
- 间距:物品之间2-5cm呼吸空间,绝不触碰或重叠
- 平衡:在画幅中均匀分布视觉重量(重物品不聚集)
- 焦点层次:中心或黄金比例放置主打产品(最大/最标志性单品)
- 边缘处理:物品完全包含在画框边界内——无裁剪

阶段4:色彩编排(自主)
自主确定[品牌名称]的标志性色板:
- 基础层:80%中性色(白色、柔和灰色、奶油色)以获得干净的编辑感
- 品牌点缀:15-20%标志性品牌色战略性地分布在物品中(标志、包装、产品细节)
- 视觉凝聚力:品牌色应至少出现在5-8个物品中以创造节奏和统一性
- 材质色板:混合哑光、光泽、金属、织物纹理以获得触感多样性,同时保持色彩纪律
- 示例:根据不同品牌调整

阶段5:相机与照明设置
- 相机角度:完美90°俯视/鸟瞰(正交透视,无镜头畸变)
- 焦距:50-80mm等效,用于自然比例表现
- 景深:f/8-f/11,用于从前到后清晰度(一切都在焦点中)
- 照明:柔和漫射顶部光源,模拟朝北窗户或大型柔光箱
- 阴影处理:物品正下方最小柔和阴影(5-10%不透明度)用于微妙接地——避免刺眼定向阴影
- 背景:纯白或浅灰无缝表面(纸张、亚克力或织物),零纹理

阶段6:造型细节
- 物品状态:原始崭新——无磨损、无做旧(收藏家心态)
- 方向:物品尽可能面向相机(标志可读、产品可识别)
- 道具真实性:使用真实产品,非模型(纹理和细节必须逼真)
- 负空间:15-25%画幅保持空白,用于视觉呼吸空间和现代编辑美学
- 无手/模特:纯产品焦点——物品自主存在

阶段7:后期处理润色
- 调色:明亮、干净、高调,略微去饱和以获得编辑精致感
- 对比度:中低对比度以避免刺眼(Instagram友好的亮度)
- 锐度:清晰但不过度锐化(自然纹理细节)
- 背景:纯白,必要时带微妙渐变(RGB 250-255范围)
- 品牌色彩流行:仅在品牌点缀色上略微提升饱和度以获得视觉冲击力

技术规格:
- 相机模拟:Phase One IQ4, 80mm镜头, f/8, ISO 100
- 宽高比:1:1(Instagram)或4:5(编辑竖向)
- 分辨率:8K用于印刷质量缩放和细节
- 风格参考:Hypebeast产品专题、Highsnobiety静物、Kinfolk杂志平铺、Supreme产品发布
- 渲染:逼真,带编辑修饰(去除灰尘,完美对齐)
- 情绪:有组织、令人向往、可收藏、现代极简主义
```

## English

```
[BRAND NAME]. Act as a Product Photographer and Art Director specializing in editorial still life and brand identity visual systems.
PHASE 1: CONCEPTUAL FRAMEWORK
Create a curated flat lay composition that visualizes brand ubiquity through everyday objects. This is a "branded universe" – a knolling-style arrangement where [BRAND NAME] colonizes ordinary life through strategic product placement. The image should feel like opening a collector's archive or a brand devotee's essentials kit.
PHASE 2: OBJECT SELECTION (AUTONOMOUS CURATION)
Autonomously select 10-15 objects that represent [BRAND NAME]'s lifestyle ecosystem:
- Category Mix:
- Object Logic by Brand Archetype:
- Size Variation: Mix large anchors (shoes, bags) with small details (keychains, matches, pills) for visual rhythm
- Brand Integration: Each object must feature [BRAND NAME] logo/branding visibly but naturally (no forced placement)
PHASE 3: KNOLLING COMPOSITION RULES
- Layout: Organized grid system with invisible alignment lines – objects parallel or perpendicular to frame edges
- Arrangement Style: Controlled chaos – appears spontaneous but follows strict geometric logic
- Spacing: 2-5cm breathing room between objects, never touching or overlapping
- Balance: Distribute visual weight evenly across frame (heavy objects don't cluster)
- Focal Hierarchy: Center or golden ratio placement for hero product (largest/most iconic item)
- Edge Treatment: Objects fully contained within frame borders – nothing cropped
PHASE 4: COLOR ORCHESTRATION (AUTONOMOUS)
Autonomously determine [BRAND NAME]'s signature color palette:
- Base Layer: 80% neutral (white, soft gray, cream) for clean editorial feel
- Brand Accent: 15-20% signature brand color strategically placed across objects (logos, packaging, product details)
- Visual Cohesion: Brand color should appear in 5-8 objects minimum to create rhythm and unity
- Material Palette: Mix matte, glossy, metallic, fabric textures for tactile variety while maintaining color discipline
- Examples:
PHASE 5: CAMERA & LIGHTING SETUP
- Camera Angle: Perfect 90° overhead/bird's eye view (orthographic perspective, no lens distortion)
- Focal Length: 50-80mm equivalent for natural scale representation
- Depth of Field: f/8-f/11 for front-to-back sharpness (everything in focus)
- Lighting: Soft, diffused overhead light source simulating north-facing window or large softbox
- Shadow Treatment: Minimal, soft shadows directly beneath objects (5-10% opacity) for subtle grounding – avoid harsh directional shadows
- Background: Pure white or light gray seamless surface (paper, acrylic, or fabric) with zero texture
PHASE 6: STYLING DETAILS
- Object Condition: Pristine and new – no wear, no distress (collector mentality)
- Orientation: Items face camera when possible (logos readable, products identifiable)
- Prop Authenticity: Use real products, not mockups (texture and detail must be photorealistic)
- Negative Space: 15-25% of frame remains empty for visual breathing room and modern editorial aesthetic
- No Hands/Models: Pure product focus – objects exist autonomously
PHASE 7: POST-PRODUCTION POLISH
- Color Grading: Bright, clean, high-key with slight desaturation for editorial sophistication
- Contrast: Medium-low contrast to avoid harshness (Instagram-friendly luminosity)
- Sharpness: Crisp but not over-sharpened (natural texture detail)
- Background: Pure white with subtle gradient if needed (RGB 250-255 range)
- Brand Color Pop: Slight saturation boost only on brand accent color for visual punch
TECHNICAL SPECS:
- Camera Simulation: Phase One IQ4, 80mm lens, f/8, ISO 100
- Aspect Ratio: 1:1 (Instagram) or 4:5 (editorial vertical)
- Resolution: 8K for print-quality zoom and detail
- Style Reference: Hypebeast product features, Highsnobiety still life, Kinfolk magazine flat lays, Supreme product drops
- Rendering: Photorealistic with editorial retouching (remove dust, perfect alignment)
- Mood: Organized, aspirational, collectible, modern minimalism
```

## 中文

这是一个三维动态图形和CGI字体艺术作品,专门设计用于展现品牌标志的日本街头服饰、Y2K赛车运动美学和复古科技硬件风格。整个设计融合了川崎赛车涂装的锐利几何、内藤泰弘标题卡的戏剧性排版、Wipeout游戏UI的未来感界面,以及Peak Design与日本硬件联名的精致工艺。

品牌标志的文字采用完全三维的物理雕塑形式,厚实而坚固,占据真实的空间深度。每个字母都有平坦的前表面,顶部和底部边缘带有三十到四十五度的倒角切割——这是在日本赛车整流罩和川崎油箱徽章上常见的角度几何。字母略微压缩且重度斜体,向前倾斜的动量内置于几何结构中。整体轮廓读起来像一个统一的空气动力学形式,字母通过底部的公共地面平面连接,仿佛一块铸造的铝件。

材质采用高光泽汽车漆面,使用指定的品牌颜色——与日本运动摩托车油箱相同的漆面系统。表面属性包括折射率1.6,粗糙度0.05,可见的清漆深度。颜色在中间色调中纯净饱和,在镜面峰值时推向近白色,在凹陷区域和倒角底面拉向深阴影。前表面上有微妙的暗环境反射,极简且情绪化,刚好足以显示材料深度。顶部倒角面捕捉主光源作为明亮高光条——这是定义三维体积的标志性细节。

一条连续的霓虹管精确追踪整个文字标识的外部轮廓线,包括每个字母边缘、每个倒角角度、字母之间的每个连接点。这不是发光效果或描边,而是物理玻璃霓虹管,直径四到六毫米,精确弯曲以在距字母表面三到五毫米的偏移距离处跟随字母形式轮廓。霓虹管本身由半透明玻璃管体构成,内部等离子体发光透过玻璃可见。光发射为明亮的冷白光,色温七千到八千开尔文,纯白色带极轻微的蓝冷偏向。发射的光在周围黑色虚空中创造紧密的光晕照明,半径八到十五毫米的柔和发光。

一个硬件屏幕面板物理集成到文字标识中,不是单独的漂浮元素。集成逻辑根据品牌标志的几何结构自主确定最自然的附着点——可能采用重叠模式、延伸模式或嵌入模式。屏幕面板由机加工铝或拉丝钢外壳构成,与字母雕塑相同的厚度,深度八到十二毫米。表面是略微磨砂的亚克力面板,从内部背光。屏幕具有与文字标识相同的斜体角度,随构图倾斜而非直立。

屏幕内容以精确的小尺度细节渲染:品牌名称以小型干净的技术无衬线字体作为主要标签,自主生成的字母数字型号代码感觉真实于品牌的文化语言,两到三个日文字符作为次要标签,水平LED进度条包含四到六个矩形段(一到两个以琥珀色点亮,其他为暗色),沿外壳一边有小通风格栅或网格图案。屏幕通过一到两根细电缆连接到霓虹轮廓系统,物理电线直径一到两毫米,受重力影响略微有机下垂。

背景是纯绝对黑色,完全虚空,无环境光,无渐变,无表面。构图自发光——唯一光源是霓虹管发射、屏幕的内部背光和单个窄顶部聚光灯。顶部聚光灯直接聚焦在文字标识中心上方,照亮顶部倒角面作为明亮高光条,在字母形式边缘五到十厘米内完全衰减,从不到达背景。

整个作品采用1:1方形宽高比,使用Octane Render或Redshift渲染,光线追踪开启用于准确的霓虹管发射、屏幕背光发光、玻璃折射和电缆阴影。体积光仅在霓虹管和屏幕表面周围微妙呈现,物理存在的发光不使黑色虚空起雾。霓虹管采用介电玻璃材料带物理光发射,屏幕表面为半透明亚克力带内部发射背光,字母雕塑为汽车漆面带完整清漆深度。抗锯齿最大,采样至少1024样本,无胶片颗粒。输出感觉像CGI标题卡或专辑封面,精确和氛围同等重要,黑暗绝对,霓虹和屏幕物理真实。

## English

This is a three-dimensional motion graphics and CGI type art work, specifically designed to showcase brand logos in Japanese streetwear, Y2K motorsport aesthetics, and retro-tech hardware style. The entire design fuses the sharp geometry of Kawasaki Racing livery, dramatic typography of Yasuhiro Nightow title cards, futuristic interface of Wipeout game UI, and refined craftsmanship of Peak Design × Japanese hardware collaborations.

The brand logo wordmark takes the form of a fully three-dimensional physical sculpture, thick and solid, occupying real spatial depth. Each letter has a flat front face, with top and bottom edges carrying thirty to forty-five degree bevel cuts—angular geometry commonly found on Japanese racing fairings and Kawasaki tank badges. Letters are slightly condensed and heavily italic, with forward momentum built into the geometric structure. The overall silhouette reads like a unified aerodynamic form, letters connecting through a common ground plane at the bottom, like a single cast aluminum piece.

The material employs high-gloss automotive lacquer, using the specified brand color—the same paint system as Japanese sports motorcycle tanks. Surface properties include refractive index 1.6, roughness 0.05, visible clear-coat depth. Color is pure and saturated in midtones, pushing toward near-white in specular peaks, pulling toward deep shadow in recessed zones and bevel undersides. Front surface carries subtle dark environment reflections, minimal and moody, just enough to show material depth. Top bevel faces catch primary light as bright highlight strips—the signature detail that defines three-dimensional volume.

A continuous neon tube precisely traces the exact outer silhouette contour of the entire wordmark, including every letter edge, every bevel angle, every connecting point between letters. This is not a glow effect or stroke, but a physical glass neon tube, four to six millimeters diameter, bent precisely to follow letterform outline at three to five millimeters offset distance from letter surface. The neon tube itself consists of semi-transparent glass tube body, internal plasma glow visible through glass. Light emission is bright cold white light, color temperature seven thousand to eight thousand Kelvin, pure white with very slight blue-cool bias. Emitted light creates tight halo illumination in surrounding black void, soft glow with eight to fifteen millimeter radius.

A hardware screen panel is physically integrated with the wordmark, not a separate floating element. Integration logic autonomously determines the most natural attachment point based on brand logo geometry—possibly adopting overlap mode, extension mode, or inset mode. Screen panel consists of machined aluminum or brushed steel housing, same thickness as letter sculpture, eight to twelve millimeters deep. Surface is slightly frosted acrylic panel, backlit from within. Screen has same italic angle as wordmark, leaning with composition rather than upright.

Screen contents are rendered in precise small-scale detail: brand name in small clean technical sans-serif as primary label, autonomously generated alphanumeric model code feeling authentic to brand's cultural language, two to three Japanese characters as secondary label, horizontal LED progress bar containing four to six rectangular segments (one to two lit in amber, others dark), small ventilation grille or mesh pattern along one edge of housing. Screen connects to neon outline system via one to two thin cables, physical wires one to two millimeters diameter, with slight organic sag from gravity.

Background is pure absolute black, complete void, no ambient, no gradient, no surface. Composition is self-illuminated—only light sources are neon tube emission, screen's internal backlight, and single narrow overhead spot. Overhead spot focuses directly above wordmark center, illuminating top bevel faces as bright highlight strips, falling off completely within five to ten centimeters of letterform edges, never reaching background.

The entire work employs 1:1 square aspect ratio, rendered using Octane Render or Redshift, ray tracing enabled for accurate neon tube emission, screen backlight glow, glass refraction, and cable shadows. Volumetric light presents subtly only around neon tube and screen surface, physically present glow without fogging black void. Neon tube uses dielectric glass material with physical light emission, screen surface is translucent acrylic with internal emissive backlight, letter sculpture is automotive lacquer with full clear-coat depth. Maximum anti-aliasing, minimum 1024 samples, no film grain. Output feels like CGI title card or album cover, precision and atmosphere in equal measure, darkness absolute, neon and screen physically real.

## English

```
A premium collectible-style cinematic diorama featuring the reference character riding a fantastic animal, designed as a high-end display piece.

The scene feels like a carefully crafted physical model photographed on a tabletop battlefield base.

Animal Design Logic (CRITICAL)

The creature must feel narratively aligned with the character.

Subtle shared accents in color temperature, surface finish, or accessory rhythm.

If the character has matte textures, the mount echoes matte surfaces.

If the character includes metallic details, the mount may carry restrained plated elements.

If the character is mystical, the mount may have subtle engraved patterns.

No costume duplication.

No literal “animal version” of the character.

Harmony, not mimicry.

Camera & Perspective

High-angle tilt-shift perspective.

Slightly offset bird’s-eye view.

Lens equivalent: 35mm with tilt-shift effect.

The character + mount fill roughly 60% of the vertical frame.

Visible base platform beneath them.

The platform must be clearly defined:

Raised edges.

Story-driven terrain surface.

Miniature battlefield texture.

No extreme wide distortion.

No centered symmetry.

Focus Discipline (CRITICAL)

Very narrow horizontal band of sharp focus.

The rider’s head and the mount’s forward torso are razor sharp.

Front hooves / limbs slightly soft.

Rear section progressively blurred.

Top and bottom of frame exhibit strong optical blur due to tilt-shift effect.

No full-body sharpness.

Lighting

Natural daylight simulation.

Directional key light from one side, clean and crisp.

Defined shadow beneath mount, clearly anchored to platform.

Subtle rim light separating silhouette from backdrop.

No dramatic volumetric beams.

No fantasy glow.

Background

Minimal studio gradient backdrop.

Soft sky-like tone.

Slight warm-to-cool gradient.

No detailed environment.

No horizon line competing with the platform.

Background supports, never competes.

Material & Surface Style

Premium collectible aesthetic.

Matte vinyl-like surfaces.

Clean edges.

Soft shadow transitions.

Subtle micro texture in focal plane.

No heavy wear.

No gritty realism.

The mount and rider feel like limited-edition statues.

Color Control

Character and mount slightly more saturated than background.

Background muted and clean.

Controlled highlight roll-off.

No neon saturation.

Emotion

Heroic.

Display-worthy.

Like a figure you’d see in a glass case at a convention.

Power, but refined.

Negative prompt:

No full-frame sharpness

No realistic gritty battlefield

No cluttered background

No symmetrical pose

No low-angle distortion

No dramatic fantasy lighting

No over-texturing

ar 4:5
```

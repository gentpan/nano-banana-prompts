## 中文

这是一个高级收藏风格的电影感立体模型,展现参考角色骑乘在一只奇幻动物上,设计为高端展示作品。整个场景给人的感觉就像是在桌面战场基座上精心制作的实体模型被拍摄下来的照片。

奇幻坐骑的设计必须在叙事上与角色保持一致。它们之间存在着微妙的共享元素——色温、表面光洁度或配饰节奏上的呼应。如果角色具有哑光质感,坐骑也会呼应哑光表面;如果角色包含金属细节,坐骑可能带有克制的镀层元素;如果角色具有神秘感,坐骑可能有精致的雕刻图案。这种和谐关系绝不是服装复制,也不是将角色直译成"动物版本",而是一种和谐共鸣,而非简单模仿。

相机采用高角度移轴透视,呈现轻微偏移的鸟瞰视角,镜头等效于带有移轴效果的35毫米。角色加坐骑大约占据垂直画幅的百分之六十,下方可见基座平台。这个平台必须清晰界定:凸起的边缘、叙事驱动的地形表面、微缩战场纹理。构图避免极端广角畸变和居中对称。

焦点纪律极其关键:只有非常窄的水平带处于锐利焦点。骑手的头部和坐骑的前躯干剃刀般锐利,前蹄或肢体略微柔化,后部逐渐模糊。由于移轴效果,画面的顶部和底部呈现强烈的光学模糊。不允许全身锐利,这种有限的焦点平面正是移轴摄影的标志。

照明采用自然日光模拟,来自一侧的定向主光源干净利落。坐骑下方有清晰的阴影,明确锚定在平台上。微妙的边缘光将轮廓与背景分离。没有戏剧性的体积光束,没有奇幻光晕,保持简洁专业的照明。

背景采用最小化的影棚渐变背景,柔和的天空般色调,轻微的暖冷渐变。没有详细的环境,没有与平台竞争的地平线。背景支持构图但从不喧宾夺主。

材质和表面风格呈现出高级收藏品美学:哑光乙烯基般的表面、干净的边缘、柔和的阴影过渡、焦平面内的微妙微观纹理。没有厚重的磨损,没有粗糙的写实主义。坐骑和骑手感觉像限量版雕像,如同你会在展会玻璃柜中看到的展示品——英雄气概但精致优雅,力量感与精炼感并存。

色彩控制上,角色和坐骑的饱和度略高于背景。背景保持柔和干净,高光呈现受控的衰减。没有霓虹饱和度,整体情绪是英雄般的、值得展示的,宛如你会在展会玻璃柜中看到的限量手办。

相机轻微抬高,高于中心五到十度,揭示顶部倒角面并确认三维深度。透视轻度,景深为零——从屏幕细节到整个构图都保持锐利(除了刻意的移轴模糊区域)。霓虹轮廓在一到两个点略微突破隐含的边界框,确认物理分离感。

整个作品采用高级收藏品风格电影感立体模型美学,呈现4:5宽高比。拒绝全画幅锐利、写实粗糙战场、杂乱背景、对称姿势、低角度畸变、戏剧性奇幻照明和过度纹理化。这是一个干净、精致、技术性和富有氛围感的微缩收藏世界。

## English

This is a premium collectible-style cinematic diorama featuring the reference character riding a fantastic animal, designed as a high-end display piece. The scene feels like a carefully crafted physical model photographed on a tabletop battlefield base.

The fantastic mount design must feel narratively aligned with the character. Between them exist subtle shared accents—echoes in color temperature, surface finish, or accessory rhythm. If the character has matte textures, the mount echoes matte surfaces; if the character includes metallic details, the mount may carry restrained plated elements; if the character is mystical, the mount may have subtle engraved patterns. This harmonic relationship is never costume duplication, nor a literal translation of the character into an "animal version," but rather harmony resonating, not simple mimicry.

The camera employs high-angle tilt-shift perspective, presenting a slightly offset bird's-eye view, lens equivalent to 35mm with tilt-shift effect. Character plus mount fill roughly sixty percent of the vertical frame, with the base platform visible beneath. This platform must be clearly defined: raised edges, story-driven terrain surface, miniature battlefield texture. Composition avoids extreme wide distortion and centered symmetry.

Focus discipline is critically important: only a very narrow horizontal band is in sharp focus. The rider's head and the mount's forward torso are razor sharp, front hooves or limbs slightly soft, rear section progressively blurred. Due to the tilt-shift effect, the top and bottom of the frame exhibit strong optical blur. No full-body sharpness is permitted—this limited focal plane is the hallmark of tilt-shift photography.

Lighting employs natural daylight simulation, directional key light from one side, clean and crisp. Defined shadow beneath the mount, clearly anchored to the platform. Subtle rim light separates silhouette from backdrop. No dramatic volumetric beams, no fantasy glow, maintaining clean professional illumination.

Background uses a minimal studio gradient backdrop with soft sky-like tone, slight warm-to-cool gradient. No detailed environment, no horizon line competing with the platform. Background supports the composition but never competes.

Material and surface style presents premium collectible aesthetic: matte vinyl-like surfaces, clean edges, soft shadow transitions, subtle micro texture in the focal plane. No heavy wear, no gritty realism. Mount and rider feel like limited-edition statues, like display pieces you'd see in a glass case at a convention—heroic yet refined and elegant, power sense coexisting with refinement.

In color control, character and mount have slightly more saturation than background. Background remains muted and clean, highlights presenting controlled roll-off. No neon saturation, overall mood is heroic, display-worthy, like limited-edition figures you'd see in convention glass cases.

Camera is slightly elevated, five to ten degrees above center, revealing top chamfer faces and confirming three-dimensional depth. Mild perspective, depth of field is zero—from screen detail to the entire composition maintaining sharpness (except deliberately blurred tilt-shift zones). Neon outline breaches the implied bounding box slightly at one to two points, confirming physical separation.

The entire work employs premium collectible-style cinematic diorama aesthetic, presenting 4:5 aspect ratio. Rejects full-frame sharpness, realistic gritty battlefield, cluttered background, symmetrical pose, low-angle distortion, dramatic fantasy lighting, and over-texturing. This is a clean, refined, technical, and atmospheric miniature collectible world.
